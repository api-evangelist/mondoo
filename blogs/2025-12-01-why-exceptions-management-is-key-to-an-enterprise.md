Security and IT teams deal with an overwhelming ‘sea’ of security alerts every day. Prioritizing issues helps teams focus on the most important issues so these can be addressed first. However, even when vulnerabilities are critical and need to be prioritized, there may still be valid reasons for not remediating them immediately.
