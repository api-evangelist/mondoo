The CSA's new whitepaper frames AI agent security as a disclosure problem, but the real issue is a decade-old remediation gap that's now exposed by AI's compressed exploit timelines, as shown by 12,000+ Flowise instances still unpatched seven months after a CVSS 10.0 fix shipped. Security leaders should stop treating AI security as a separate ML governance silo and instead measure mean-time-to-remediation across their full AI stack, since most agentic vulnerabilities are conventional flaws (RCE, SQLi, path traversal) in new component classes.
