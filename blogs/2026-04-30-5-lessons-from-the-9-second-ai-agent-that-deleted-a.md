On April 25, 2026, an AI coding agent deleted a production database and its backups in nine seconds. The real failures were not about AI going rogue but about overprivileged tokens, shared blast radius between backups and primary data, missing environment isolation, and absent destructive-action gates. Five concrete lessons drawn from the public post-mortem show what actually prevents the next incident.
