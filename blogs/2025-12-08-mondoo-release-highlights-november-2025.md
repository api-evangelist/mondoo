November has already flown by, and it’s time to take a look at all the enhancements we accomplished. This month we added new Mondoo Security Advisories designed to keep you ahead of emerging threats. We also expanded our vulnerability detection to include more applications and operating systems, as well as guided remediation steps to help you patch quickly.
