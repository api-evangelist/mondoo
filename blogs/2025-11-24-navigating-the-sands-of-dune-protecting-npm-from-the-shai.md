In Frank Herbert's science fiction classic "Dune", the Shai-Hulud, a massive sandworm, strikes unpredictably beneath the sands of the desert planet Arrakis. In 2025, a digital version appeared: the Shai-Hulud Node Package Manager (NPM) worm. This self-replicating malware infiltrates the NPM supply chain, stealing credentials and causing chaos across numerous repositories.
