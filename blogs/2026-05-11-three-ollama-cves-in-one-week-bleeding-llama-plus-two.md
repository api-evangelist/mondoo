Three distinct Ollama vulnerabilities surfaced in early May 2026 and most coverage runs them together. CVE-2026-7482 (Bleeding Llama) is a critical unauthenticated heap memory leak in the GGUF loader, fixed in 0.17.1. CVE-2026-42248 and CVE-2026-42249 are two Windows-only auto-updater bugs that chain into persistent code execution.
