The Shai-Hulud worm compromised 796 packages with 132 million monthly downloads. npm has made real progress on supply chain security, but registry-side defenses alone are not enough. pnpm ships client-side controls that block lifecycle scripts, quarantine new releases, and detect credential compromise.
