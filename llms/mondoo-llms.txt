# Mondoo

> Mondoo provides Agentic Vulnerability Management - AI-powered security that discovers, prioritizes, and remediates vulnerabilities automatically. We don't just tell you what's wrong. We actually fix it.

Mondoo is trusted by Fortune 50 companies including Deutsche Telekom and major US automotive manufacturers for Agentic Vulnerability Management at scale.
